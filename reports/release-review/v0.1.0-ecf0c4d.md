### G5 · why names are not final

`docs/cardText-naming.md` does not exist, but
[`docs/cardContainer-naming.md`](https://github.com/Aye-Kyawt/horizon-stays-design-system/blob/ecf0c4d246935bf010a17b8e3288fcaf1dd430f0/docs/cardContainer-naming.md)

Its section *"One conflict, and the name proposed for it"*

> **Alternative, if design prefers:** rename the Figma boolean to `showPrice` (and `showReview`,

> three. That is a design-file change, so it is a suggestion, not something done here.

The suggestion is not cosmetic. If design takes it, three of `cardText`'s boolean props are renamed

## What blocks this release

One finding blocks both components, and it is not local to either:

**G3 cannot be cleared one component at a time.** `npm test` is a whole-repo run. At this commit it
fails on **8 of the 11 component stylesheets** — `Button.css` (9 reads), `cardText.css` (7),
`chip.css`, `spinner.css` (6 each), `iconBtn.css` (5), `tooltip.css` (4), `avatar.css` and
`progressBar.css` (1 each). Only `cardContainer`, `cardImage` and `cardLayout` are clean. Because the
gate requires a passing test run *as well as* a clean stylesheet, **no component in this repo can be
Cleared while any of those eight still reads a primitive** — including the five not under review
here. `docs/button-design-gaps.md` reaches the same conclusion at its close, from the engineering
side.

Clearing this release therefore needs, in order:

1. **Design decides the token gaps.** `docs/button-design-gaps.md` and
   `docs/cardContainer-design-gaps.md` name what is missing: `component/button/icon/{hovered,disabled}`,
   `component/button/border/hovered`, a 10px button `padding/y`, a focus-ring offset role, a button-label
   weight token, a body-semibold type token, and the absent `semantic/size/*` layer. Adding tokens is not
   the engineer's call and was correctly not done.
2. **Engineer repoints the eight stylesheets** at the new semantic tokens, and QA re-tests — the values
   must not move, or the staging rows that passed against the Figma nodes no longer hold.
3. **doc-generator writes `cardText.intent.json`**, which clears G1 and makes C1, C3, C4, C5 and C6
   evaluable for `cardText`.
4. **Design records a decision on the `showPrice`/`showReview`/`showMetadata` suggestion**, which
   clears G5 for `cardText`.

Steps 3 and 4 are `cardText`'s alone. Step 1 and 2 are the whole repo's.
