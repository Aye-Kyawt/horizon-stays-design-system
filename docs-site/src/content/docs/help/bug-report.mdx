---
title: Report a bug
description: How to report a bug in the Horizon Stays design system.
---

Open an issue on the repository:
**https://github.com/Aye-Kyawt/horizon-stays-design-system/issues/new**

## What to include

- **The package version.** Check it with `npm ls @aye_kyawt/horizon-stays-design-system`.
- **The component, and the props you passed.**
- **The theme:** light or dark.
- **What you expected, and what you saw.** A screenshot is best.
- **Whether Storybook shows it too.** Find the matching story in the
  [production Storybook](https://horizon-stays-storybook-production.vercel.app) and include its
  URL. If the story looks right and your app doesn't, include how you load `tokens.css` and
  `styles.css`.

## Before you file

- Check that both stylesheets load, **tokens first**. A component with no tokens renders without
  its colours.
- Check `label`: `Button` does not render children.
